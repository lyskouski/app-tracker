## Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24

Thank you for using our application ("Zwyka", the "App"). This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data and
ensure your privacy while using the App. We are committed to protecting your personal information and providing
transparency about the data we collect and how it is used.

### Data Collection and Usage

The App does not collect any user data, personal information, or sensitive data. We do not require you to sign up or
provide any personal details to use the App's features. The App does not contain any advertising, analytics, or
tracking services.

### Data Storage and Security

All data entered into the App (habits, their marks, schedules, and settings) is stored locally on your device, or in
the storage of your web browser for the web version. We do not store any of your data on our servers. You are able to
export your data into a file and import it back; such files are stored only where you choose.

### Notifications

The App can show reminders for the scheduled habits. Reminders are created and delivered locally on your device by its
operating system, without sending your data to us. On Android, the App requests permissions to show notifications, to
schedule exact alarms, and to restore reminders after the device restart.

### Third-Party Services

The App can add a scheduled habit to an external calendar (Apple, Google, Microsoft) only when you choose to do so.
In that case, the title, time, and note of the habit are passed to the selected service, and its privacy policy
applies. The web version of the App is hosted by third-party providers (GitHub Pages, terCAD), which may process
technical information (such as an IP address) to deliver the App. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of
any third-party services you may access through the App.

### Updates to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. Any changes to the
policy will be posted within the App. By continuing to use the App after any modifications to the Privacy Policy,
you agree to be bound by the updated terms.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the App's data practices,
please contact us at https://github.com/lyskouski/app-tracker/issues

### Conclusion

Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and security of your data
while using the App. Rest assured that we do not collect or store any personal information. By using the App, you
agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Thank you for trusting us with your privacy while using our App.
